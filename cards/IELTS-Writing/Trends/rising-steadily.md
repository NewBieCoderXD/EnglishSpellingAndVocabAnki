# Front

Complete the sentence with <b>TWO</b> words (a present participle phrase):

<span class="ex">"The chart shows a trend of online shopping __________ , especially among younger people."</span>

<div class="hint">verb(-ing) + adverb &middot; going up gradually, without stopping</div>

---

# Type

rising steadily, increasing steadily, climbing steadily, growing steadily

---

# Back

The chart shows a trend of online shopping **<span class="ans">rising steadily</span>**, especially among younger people.

---

**rise steadily** <span class="pos">verb phrase</span> &middot; /raɪz ˈsted.əl.i/

To increase gradually and consistently.

<span class="ex">Demand has risen steadily over the past five years.</span>

*Tip: "raise" ≠ "rise" — "rise" has no object. Say "the figure rose", never "the figure raised".*
