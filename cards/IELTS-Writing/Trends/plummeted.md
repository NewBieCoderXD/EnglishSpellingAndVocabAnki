**plummet** <span class="pos">verb</span> &middot; /ˈplʌm.ɪt/

To fall extremely quickly and by a large amount.

<span class="ex">Sales plummeted to their lowest level in a decade.</span>

*Tip: a steeper word than "declined" — use for a sudden, dramatic crash in a graph.*
