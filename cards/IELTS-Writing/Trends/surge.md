# Front

Complete the sentence with <b>ONE</b> word (a noun):

<span class="ex">"The number of online purchases saw a sharp __________ between 2015 and 2020."</span>

<div class="hint">noun &middot; a sudden, large increase</div>

---

# Type

surge, increase, rise, jump, leap

---

# Back

The number of online purchases saw a sharp **<span class="ans">surge</span>** between 2015 and 2020.

---

**surge** <span class="pos">noun / verb</span> &middot; /sɜːdʒ/

A sudden, strong increase.

<span class="ex">Share prices surged on the back of the announcement.</span>

*Tip: quickly + surge / dramatic + rise. For IELTS task 1, describe sharp *, then the year.*
