# Front

Complete the sentence with <b>TWO</b> words (a verb phrase):

<span class="ex">"Emissions ____________ in 2019 and have fallen since."</span>

<div class="hint">verb + noun &middot; reached its highest point</div>

---

# Type

reached a peak, peaked, hit a peak

---

# Back

Emissions **<span class="ans">reached a peak</span>** in 2019 and have fallen since.

---

**reach a peak** <span class="pos">verb phrase</span>

To hit the highest point on a graph.

<span class="ex">The figure peaked at 40 million units in 2008.</span>

*Tip: synonyms — reach a peak / hit a high / peak at. The single-word answer here is "peaked" too: "Emissions **peaked** in 2019".*
