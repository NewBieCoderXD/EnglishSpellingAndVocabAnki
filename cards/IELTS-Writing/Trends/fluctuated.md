# Front

Complete the sentence with <b>ONE</b> word (a verb):

<span class="ex">"The unemployment rate __________ between 4% and 6% throughout the decade."</span>

<div class="hint">past simple verb &middot; to move up and down</div>

# Type

# Back

The unemployment rate **<span class="ans">fluctuated</span>** between 4% and 6% throughout the decade.

---

**fluctuate** <span class="pos">verb</span> &middot; /ˈflʌk.tʃu.eɪt/

To rise and fall irregularly; to keep changing.

<span class="ex">Prices fluctuated wildly during the crisis.</span>

*Tip: use when a line goes up and down without a clear trend.*
