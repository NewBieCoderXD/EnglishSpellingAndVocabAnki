# Front

Complete the sentence with <b>ONE</b> word (a verb):

<span class="ex">"Sales __________ steeply during the final quarter of the year."</span>

<div class="hint">past simple verb &middot; opposite of "rose"</div>

# Type

declined, dropped, fell, dipped, plummeted

---

# Back

Sales **<span class="ans">declined</span>** steeply during the final quarter of the year.

---

**decline** <span class="pos">verb / noun</span> &middot; /dɪˈklaɪn/

To become smaller or weaker; a decrease.

<span class="ex">The population declined by 10% over the decade.</span>

*Tip: decline + dramatically / sharply / steadily — avoid "reduce went down".*
