---

**level off** <span class="pos">verb phrase</span> &middot; /ˈlev.əl ɒf/

To stop increasing or decreasing and remain steady.

<span class="ex">The number of users levelled off at about 2,000.</span>

*Tip: synonyms — levelled off / plateaued / flattened out / stabilised.*
