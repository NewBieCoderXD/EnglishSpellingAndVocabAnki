<span class="ex">"South America had a highly uneven distribution of causes."</span>
<span class="ex">"There was a considerable disparity among the different causes."</span>

*Tip: don't use **disparity** for every difference &mdash; it implies something worth complaining about. For plain arithmetic, **difference** is correct and safer.*
