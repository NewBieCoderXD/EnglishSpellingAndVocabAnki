---

<div class="pat">a disparity <b>in</b> + noun &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; a disparity <b>between</b> A and B</div>

* **disparity in income / disparity in access** &mdash; the spread of one thing across a group.
* **disparity between the two regions** &mdash; the gap between two things.

*Tip: use <b>in</b> when one quantity is unevenly spread; <b>between</b> when you are comparing two named things. Task 2: "a stark disparity in access to healthcare".*
