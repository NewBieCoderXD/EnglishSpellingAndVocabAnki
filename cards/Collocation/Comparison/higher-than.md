# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_COMPARISON &middot; comparative + <b>than</b></div>

Type the missing word:

<span class="ex">"South America's figure was significantly ____________ Central Africa's (70% vs 30%)."</span>

---

# Type

higher, greater, larger, bigger, more

---

# Back

South America's figure was significantly **<span class="ans">higher</span>** than Central Africa's.

---

<div class="pat">X was higher / lower <b>than</b> Y &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; X was more than 70%</div>

* higher / lower / greater &mdash; figures
* larger / smaller &mdash; areas, populations, physical size
* more / fewer &mdash; countable items: "more students enrolled"

*Tip: <b>than</b> for comparisons, <b>of</b> for a share: "70% of the total" but "higher than 30%".*
