# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_COMPARISON &middot; not "compared <b>than</b>"</div>

Type the missing words:

<span class="ex">"The figure was considerably higher ____________ the previous year."</span>

---

# Type

compared with, compared to

---

# Back

The figure was considerably higher **<span class="ans">compared with</span>** the previous year.

---

<div class="pat">higher / lower <b>compared with (to)</b> + baseline</div>

<span class="ex">Sales were significantly higher compared with the corresponding period in 2010.</span>

*Tip: British English prefers **compared with**, American English **compared to** &mdash; both are safe. Simpler and just as accurate: "The figure was considerably higher than in the previous year."*
