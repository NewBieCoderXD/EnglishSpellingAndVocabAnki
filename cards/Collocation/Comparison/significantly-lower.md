# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_COMPARISON &middot; how big is the difference?</div>

Type the missing words:

<span class="ex">"Central Africa's figure was ____________ than South America's (30% vs 70%)."</span>

---

# Type

significantly lower, considerably lower, substantially lower, much lower, far lower, lower

---

# Back

Central Africa's figure was **<span class="ans">significantly lower</span>** than South America's.

---

<div class="pat">significantly / considerably / substantially <b>lower</b> than</div>

* **small gap:** slightly, marginally, marginally lower
* **clear gap:** significantly, considerably, substantially, markedly
* **big gap:** dramatically / vastly / far lower

*Tip: the adverb carries the judgement. Never write just "very lower".*
