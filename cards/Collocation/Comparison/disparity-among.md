# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_COMPARISON &middot; <b>among</b> for 3+ items, <b>between</b> for 2</div>

Type the missing noun:

<span class="ex">"There was a considerable ____________ among the different causes of deforestation."</span>

---

# Type

disparity, difference, variation, gap, imbalance

---

# Back

There was a considerable **<span class="ans">disparity</span>** among the different causes of deforestation.

---

<div class="pat">disparity <b>between</b> two &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; disparity <b>among</b> three or more</div>

<span class="ex">There was a marked variation among the three regions.</span>

*Tip: **among** is a little more formal than **between**, so it reads well in Task 1. **Variation among** is also fine when you are comparing several figures without implying unfairness.*
