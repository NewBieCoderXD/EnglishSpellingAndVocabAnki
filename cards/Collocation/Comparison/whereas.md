# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_COMPARISON &middot; joins two contrasting facts</div>

Type the missing word:

<span class="ex">"South America recorded 70%, ____________ Central Africa recorded 30%."</span>

---

# Type

whereas, while, whilst

---

# Back

South America recorded 70%, **<span class="ans">whereas</span>** Central Africa recorded 30%.

---

<div class="pat">A recorded 70%, <b>whereas</b> B recorded 30%</div>

* **whereas / whilst** &mdash; a clear contrast between two facts; formal, so ideal for Task 1.
* **while** &mdash; the same job, slightly more conversational.

*Tip: <b>however</b> is a full stop, not a joining word &mdash; <span class="no">70%, however Central Africa 30%</span>. And "A, while B" is fine, but don't stack: <span class="no">whereas while</span>.*
