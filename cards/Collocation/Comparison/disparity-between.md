# Type

# Back

There was a considerable **<span class="ans">disparity</span>** between the two regions.

---

<div class="pat">a disparity <b>between</b> A and B &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; a disparity <b>in</b> X</div>

* **difference** &mdash; any numerical gap, positive or not, however small.
* **disparity** &mdash; a noticeable, meaningful, usually unfair gap.
* **gap / divide** &mdash; also implies distance between levels.

*Tip: <b>disparity</b> needs a gap worth naming: <span class="no">a disparity between 51% and 50%</span> would sound ridiculous.*
