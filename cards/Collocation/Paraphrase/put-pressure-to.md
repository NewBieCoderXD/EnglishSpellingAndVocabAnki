# Back

Rising energy prices put pressure **<span class="ans">on** household budgets.

---

<div class="pat">put pressure <b>on</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; put a strain <b>on</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; place a burden <b>on</b></div>

* **put pressure on** (no article) &mdash; general stress.
* **put a strain on** (with an article) &mdash; pushing a system to its limit.
* **place a burden on** &mdash; a heavy, often financial, load.

*Tip: <span class="no">put pressure to</span> &middot; <span class="no">put a pressure on</span> &middot; <span class="no">cause pressure on</span>. One form, always **on**.*
