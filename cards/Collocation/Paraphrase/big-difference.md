<span class="ex">"There was a big difference between the two regions. &nbsp;&rarr; &nbsp;There was a substantial ____________ between the two regions."</span>

# Type

disparity, difference, gap, variation, divide

---

# Back

There was a substantial **<span class="ans">disparity** between the two regions.

---

<div class="pat">a substantial / marked / stark <b>disparity</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; a significant difference</div>

* **disparity** &mdash; a meaningful, usually unfair gap: "a substantial disparity in income".
* **difference** &mdash; plain arithmetic: "a significant difference between the two figures".
* **gap** &mdash; a difference in levels: "the gap between rich and poor narrowed".

*Tip: **disparity** carries a judgement, so reach for it only when the gap is real and worth naming. Adjectives that fit: substantial, marked, stark, growing, widening.*
