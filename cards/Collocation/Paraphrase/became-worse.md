# Front

<div class="hint">PARAPHRASE &middot; "became worse" &rarr; the academic collocation</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"The problem became worse over the decade. &nbsp;&rarr; &nbsp;The problem ____________."</span>

---

# Type

worsened, deteriorated, became increasingly severe, intensified, escalated

---

# Back

The problem **<span class="ans">worsened** over the decade.

---

<div class="pat">the problem <b>worsened</b> / <b>deteriorated</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; became increasingly severe</div>

* **worsened / deteriorated** &mdash; got worse: "Conditions deteriorated sharply."
* **become increasingly severe** &mdash; got worse and worse: "The problem has become increasingly severe."
* **intensified / escalated** &mdash; for pressure, conflict, competition.

*Tip: keep the subject straight. A PROBLEM worsens; a POLICY or a change exacerbates it.*
