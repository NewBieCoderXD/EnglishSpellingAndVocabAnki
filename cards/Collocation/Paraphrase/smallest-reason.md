# Front

<div class="hint">PARAPHRASE &middot; "the least impactful cause" &rarr; the academic collocation</div>

Type the missing words:

<span class="ex">"Logging was the least impactful cause of forest loss." &nbsp;&rarr; &nbsp;"Logging was the ____________ cause of forest loss."</span>

---

# Type

least significant, smallest, minor, negligible

---

# Back

Logging was the **<span class="ans">least significant** cause of forest loss.

---

<div class="pat">the least significant cause &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; the smallest proportion <b>of</b> X &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; a minor factor</div>

* **the least significant cause** &mdash; the least important one.
* **the smallest proportion** &mdash; when you are talking about the bars or the numbers.
* **a minor factor** &mdash; a single small contributor.

*Tip: <span class="no">the least impactful causes</span> is not a phrase you would say. Name the cause, or rank it.*
