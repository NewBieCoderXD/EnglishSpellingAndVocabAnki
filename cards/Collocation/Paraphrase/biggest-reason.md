# Front

<div class="hint">PARAPHRASE &middot; "the biggest reason" &rarr; the academic collocation</div>

Type the missing words:

<span class="ex">"Cattle ranching was the biggest reason for the loss of forest cover." &nbsp;&rarr; &nbsp;"Cattle ranching was the ____________ of the loss of forest cover."</span>

---

# Type

dominant cause, main cause, leading cause, primary cause, dominant factor, main factor

---

# Back

Cattle ranching was the **<span class="ans">dominant cause** of the loss of forest cover.

---

<div class="pat">the dominant / main / leading cause <b>of</b> X</div>

* **the dominant cause** &mdash; the strongest, the one that dominates.
* **the main cause** &mdash; the general one.
* **the leading cause** &mdash; formal; also used for companies ("a leading manufacturer").

*Tip: <span class="no">the biggest reason</span> &rarr; <span class="ok">the main / leading / dominant cause</span>; <span class="no">the most dominant reason</span> &rarr; <span class="ok">the dominant cause</span> (no **most**).*
