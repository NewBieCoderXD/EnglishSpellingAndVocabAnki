# Front

<div class="hint">PARAPHRASE &middot; restructure "70% was because of X"</div>

Type the correct verb:

<span class="ex">"70% of the loss was because of cattle ranching. &nbsp;&rarr; &nbsp;Cattle ranching ____________ 70% of the loss."</span>

---

# Type

accounted for, made up, constituted, was responsible for

---

# Back

Cattle ranching **<span class="ans">accounted for** 70% of the loss.

---

<div class="pat">X <b>accounted for</b> 70% of Y &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Y was largely <b>due to</b> X</div>

* **X accounted for 70% of Y** &mdash; make the cause the subject; shorter and more academic.
* **70% of Y was due to X** &mdash; keep the percentage as the subject.
* **Y was largely the result of X** &mdash; the causal chain version.

*Tip: "was because of" is not academic. Use **was due to**, **was largely attributed to**, or restructure with **accounted for**.*
