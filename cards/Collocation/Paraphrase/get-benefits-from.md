# Front

<div class="hint">PARAPHRASE &middot; your instinct &rarr; the natural IELTS collocation</div>

Type the missing phrase:

<span class="ex">"Children ____________ a great deal from structured reading sessions."</span>

---

# Type

benefit from, gain from, profit from

---

# Back

Children **<span class="ans">benefit from** a great deal from structured reading sessions.

---

<div class="pat">benefit <b>from</b> + noun / -ing</div>

* **benefit from** &mdash; the safe choice, in both Task 1 and Task 2.
* **gain from** &mdash; to obtain something: "Countries gain from trade." (*gain* needs a direct object: "gain access to …")
* **profit from** &mdash; to earn a financial benefit: "Firms profit from lower costs."

*Tip: <span class="no">get benefits from</span> is clumsy &mdash; a countable **benefit** needs a determiner, and **get** is too informal for academic writing. **Benefit from** is a single inseparable verb.*
