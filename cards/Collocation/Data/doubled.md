The yield **<span class="ans">doubled</span>** between 2000 and 2010.

---

<div class="pat">the yield <b>doubled</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; the yield was <b>twice as high as</b> that of 2000</div>

**double** <span class="pos">verb</span> &mdash; to become or make 2&times; as much

* **The yield doubled.**
* **The yield was twice as high as in the previous year.**
* **Output more than doubled.**

*Tip: <span class="no">increased by twice</span> is wrong, and <span class="no">two times higher than</span> is ambiguous (2&times; or 3&times;?) &mdash; avoid both in IELTS.*
