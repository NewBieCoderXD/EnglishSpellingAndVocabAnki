# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; noun + <b>of</b> &middot; how much of the whole</div>

Type the missing noun:

<span class="ex">"____________ of forest loss was attributed to cattle ranching."</span>

---

# Type

The proportion, The percentage, The share, The amount, Most

---

# Back

**<span class="ans">The proportion</span>** of forest loss was attributed to cattle ranching.

---

<div class="pat">the proportion / percentage / share <b>of</b> X</div>

<span class="ex">The proportion of students using public transport increased from 30% to 45%.</span>

*Tip: <b>of</b> here (a share of something), but <b>in</b> for a change: <i>an increase <b>in</b> demand</i>.*
