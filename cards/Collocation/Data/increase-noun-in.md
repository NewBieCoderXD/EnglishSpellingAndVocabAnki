# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; <b>NOUN + in</b> &middot; a change in something</div>

Type the missing noun:

<span class="ex">"There was a considerable ____________ in the number of cars on the roads."</span>

---

# Type

increase, rise, growth, jump, surge, upturn, climb

---

# Back

There was a considerable **<span class="ans">increase</span>** in the number of cars on the roads.

---

<div class="pat">an increase / rise / growth <b>in</b> + noun &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; a reduction / decline / drop <b>in</b> + noun</div>

* **weak:** growth, increase, rise, fall, drop
* **strong:** sharp increase, dramatic rise, marked decline, steep fall
* **very strong:** surge, upsurge, plummet, soar

*Tip: the noun takes <b>in</b>, not <b>of</b> &mdash; <span class="no">an increase of the number of cars</span>. A change in a quantity = <b>in</b>; a share of a total = <b>of</b>.*
