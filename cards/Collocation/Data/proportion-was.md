# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; verb after "the proportion of &hellip;"</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"The proportion of forest loss attributed to cattle ranching ____________ 70%."</span>

---

# Type

was, reached, amounted to, stood at, hit, climbed to

---

# Back

The proportion of forest loss attributed to cattle ranching **<span class="ans">was</span>** 70%.

---

<div class="pat">the proportion of X <b>was / reached</b> 70%</div>

**be** for stating a value &mdash; **reach / stand at / amount to** for arriving at it

<span class="ex">The proportion reached 70% in 2010 and remained stable thereafter.</span>

*Tip: "amounted to" and "stood at" are safely formal: "The figure amounted to 70%."*
