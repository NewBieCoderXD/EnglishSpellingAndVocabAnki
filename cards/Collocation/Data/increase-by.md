# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; <b>by</b> = the amount of change &middot; 30% &rarr; 50%</div>

Type the missing preposition:

<span class="ex">"The figure increased ____________ 20 percentage points."</span>

---

# Type

by, by nearly, by over, by some

---

# Back

The figure increased **<span class="ans">by</span>** 20 percentage points.

---

<div class="pat">increased <b>by</b> 20% &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; rose <b>by</b> two percentage points</div>

<span class="ex">The figure increased by 20% between 2000 and 2010.</span>

*Tip: for percentages, **by 20 percentage points** is unambiguous; "increased by 20%" is a relative rise. Say which you mean.*
