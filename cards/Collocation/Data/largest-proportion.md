# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; superlative before "proportion"</div>

Type the missing word:

<span class="ex">"Commercial agriculture accounted for the ____________ proportion of forest loss."</span>

---

# Type

largest, greatest, highest, biggest

---

# Back

Commercial agriculture accounted for the **<span class="ans">largest</span>** proportion of forest loss.

---

<div class="pat">the largest / smallest proportion <b>of</b> X</div>

<span class="ex">Other causes accounted for the smallest proportion of the total.</span>

*Tip: superlative adjective + <b>proportion</b> &mdash; <span class="no">the most proportion</span> is wrong. For the biggest factor, say <span class="ok">the dominant cause</span> or <span class="ok">the leading cause</span>.*
