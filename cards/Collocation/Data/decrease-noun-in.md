# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; <b>NOUN + in</b> &middot; going down</div>

Type the missing noun:

<span class="ex">"There was a steep ____________ in sales during the final quarter."</span>

---

# Type

decline, drop, fall, decrease, reduction, dip, downturn

---

# Back

There was a steep **<span class="ans">decline</span>** in sales during the final quarter.

---

<div class="pat">a decline / drop / fall / reduction <b>in</b> + noun</div>

* **decline / fall / drop** &mdash; the everyday Task 1 nouns.
* **reduction** &mdash; more formal, common with amounts: "a reduction in costs of 12%".
* **decrease** &mdash; correct, but the plainest of the set.

*Tip: <b>reduce</b> is the verb, <b>reduction</b> the noun: "Sales fell sharply", not <span class="no">Sales reduced</span>.*
