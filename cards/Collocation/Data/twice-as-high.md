# Type

# Back

Output in 2020 was **<span class="ans">twice</span>** as high as in 2000.

---

<div class="pat">twice / three times <b>as high as</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; twice as many as (countable)</div>

<span class="ex">The figure for 2010 was three times as high as that for 2000.</span>

*Tip: use <b>as … as</b> for an exact multiple: "twice as high as". Use <b>times higher than</b> only if you accept the ambiguity, and prefer <span class="ok">X was twice as high as Y</span>.*
