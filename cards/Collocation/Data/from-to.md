# Back

The number of students using public transport increased **<span class="ans">from 30% to 50%</span>**.

---

<div class="pat">increased <b>from</b> 30% <b>to</b> 50% &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; increased <b>by</b> 20 percentage points</div>

<span class="ex">The figure rose from 12% in 1990 to 48% in 2020.</span>

*Tip: <b>from</b> &rarr; <b>to</b> is the safest way to report a change in Task 1: it cannot be misread as a relative increase. The equivalent with <b>by</b> is the amount of change (percentage points), not the new value.*
