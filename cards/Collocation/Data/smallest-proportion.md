# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; superlative before "proportion" &middot; the opposite</div>

Type the missing word:

<span class="ex">"Other factors accounted for the ____________ proportion of forest loss."</span>

---

# Type

smallest, lowest, least significant, negligible

---

# Back

Other factors accounted for the **<span class="ans">smallest</span>** proportion of forest loss.

---

<div class="pat">the least significant / smallest cause &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; the smallest proportion</div>

<span class="ex">Logging was the least significant of the three causes.</span>

*Tip: for "the least important factor" write <span class="ok">the least significant causes</span> or <span class="ok">the causes that accounted for the smallest proportions</span> &mdash; <span class="no">the least impactful causes</span> is not a phrase you would say.*
