# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; verb + <b>X%</b> &middot; = "made up"</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"Cattle ranching ____________ 70% of forest loss in the region."</span>

---

# Type

accounted for, made up, constituted, represented, amounted to

---

# Back

Cattle ranching **<span class="ans">accounted for</span>** 70% of forest loss in the region.

---

<div class="pat">X <b>accounted for</b> 70% of Y</div>

<span class="ex">Government funding accounted for the largest proportion of the total.</span>

Four verbs, one meaning &mdash; "was equal to that share":

* **accounted for** &mdash; the everyday choice, and the safest.
* **made up** &mdash; "Cattle ranching made up 70% of forest loss."
* **constituted** &mdash; formal: "Crop burning constituted 20% of the total."
* **represented** &mdash; used with a figure as subject: "The figure represented 70% of the total."

*Tip: 70% <b>of</b> something &mdash; never "of the 70%".*
