# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; a fast, large increase &middot; noun + in</div>

Type the missing noun:

<span class="ex">"The graph shows a sharp ____________ in demand after 2015."</span>

---

# Type

surge, rise, increase, upsurge, jump, spike, climb

---

# Back

The graph shows a sharp **<span class="ans">surge</span>** in demand after 2015.

---

<div class="pat">growth <b>&lt;</b> increase / rise <b>&lt;</b> sharp increase <b>&lt;</b> surge / upsurge</div>

<span class="ex">Demand surged in the second half of the decade.</span>

*Tip: **surge** is a noun *and* a verb, and it is already "sudden and large" &mdash; <span class="no">a sharp surge</span> doubles up. <b>Upsurge** is the formal noun. **Spike** suits a single vertical line on a chart.*
