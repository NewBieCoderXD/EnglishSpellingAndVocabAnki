# Front

<div class="hint">PREPOSITION &middot; <span class="no">contribute <b>for</b></span></div>

Type the preposition:

<span class="ex">"Population growth contributed ____________ the increase in housing demand."</span>

---

# Type

to

---

# Back

Population growth contributed **<span class="ans">to** the increase in housing demand.

---

<div class="pat">contribute <b>to</b> + noun / -ing &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; lead to / result in + noun / -ing</div>

* **contribute to** &mdash; one of several causes.
* **lead to / result in** &mdash; the whole effect.

*Tip: <span class="no">contributed for</span> is wrong. After **to**, use a noun or an <b>-ing</b> form: "contributed to <i>reducing</i> emissions" &mdash; <span class="no">contributed to reduce emissions</span>.*
