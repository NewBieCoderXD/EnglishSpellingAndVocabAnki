# Back

There was a clear difference **<span class="ans">between** the two regions.

---

<div class="pat">a difference / disparity / gap <b>between</b> A and B &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; <b>among</b> three or more &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; a difference <b>in</b> + noun</div>

* **between** two: "a difference between the two regions", "a disparity between rich and poor".
* **among** three or more: "a variation among the three causes".
* **in** a single thing's spread: "a difference in income levels", "a disparity in access to healthcare".

*Tip: **between** for two, **among** for many. <span class="ok">Between men and women, the figure was higher</span> is also natural Task 1 shorthand for comparison.*
