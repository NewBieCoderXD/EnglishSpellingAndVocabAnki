# Front

<div class="hint">PREPOSITION &middot; what people want &middot; <span class="no">demand <b>of</b> housing</span></div>

Type the preposition:

<span class="ex">"There was a growing demand ____________ affordable housing."</span>

---

# Type

for

---

# Back

There was a growing demand **<span class="ans">for** affordable housing.

---

<div class="pat">demand <b>for</b> + noun &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; meet / satisfy / respond to demand <b>for</b> + noun</div>

* **demand for** &mdash; the need: "an increase in demand for housing".
* **meet / satisfy demand for** &mdash; to supply enough: "The government must meet demand for affordable homes".
* **respond to demand** &mdash; to react to it: "Firms responded quickly to rising demand".

*Tip: <span class="no">demand of housing</span>. Careful too: <b>demand</b> (what people want) and <b>request / call for</b> (someone's appeal) are different words.*
