# Front

<div class="hint">PREPOSITION &middot; no article &middot; <span class="no">put pressure <b>to</b></span></div>

Type the preposition:

<span class="ex">"The policy puts pressure ____________ natural resources."</span>

---

# Type

on, upon

---

# Back

The policy puts pressure **<span class="ans">on** natural resources.

---

<div class="pat">put pressure <b>on</b> + no article &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; put a strain <b>on</b> + with an article</div>

* **put pressure on** (no article): "The government should put pressure on emitters."
* **put a strain on** (with an article): "This puts a strain on the health service."

*Tip: <span class="no">put pressure to</span> &middot; <span class="no">put a pressure on</span> &middot; <span class="no">cause pressure on</span> &mdash; all wrong. **On** in every case.*
