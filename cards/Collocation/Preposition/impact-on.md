# Front

<div class="hint">PREPOSITION &middot; = effect / consequence &middot; <b>on</b> is fixed</div>

Type the preposition:

<span class="ex">"Globalisation has had a significant impact ____________ the environment."</span>

---

# Type

on, upon

---

# Back

Globalisation has had a significant impact **<span class="ans">on</span>** the environment.

---

<div class="pat">have an impact / effect / influence <b>on</b> + thing affected</div>

* **impact on** &mdash; a large, noticeable effect: "have a significant impact on the environment".
* **effect on** &mdash; any consequence, often negative: "have a negative effect on productivity".
* **influence on** &mdash; what shapes behaviour or a system: "have a strong influence on people's behaviour".

*Tip: <b>on</b>, never <span class="no">to</span> or <span class="no">for</span>. In Task 2 the natural sentence is "…has a significant impact <b>on</b> X", or "X has <b>affected</b> …" without a noun.*
