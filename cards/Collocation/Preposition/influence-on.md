# Front

<div class="hint">PREPOSITION &middot; = what shapes behaviour &middot; <b>on</b></div>

Type the preposition:

<span class="ex">"Advertising has a considerable influence ____________ consumer behaviour."</span>

---

# Type

on, upon

---

# Back

Advertising has a considerable influence **<span class="ans">on** consumer behaviour.

---

<div class="pat">have an influence <b>on</b> + noun / -ing &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; <b>influence</b> someone <b>to</b> do</div>

<span class="ex">Family income has a strong influence on the number of children in education.</span>

*Tip: <b>influence on</b> for the effect on a thing; <b>influence someone to do</b> for the verb frame: "Parents influence their children to study harder".*
