# Back

New providers are being encouraged to meet demand **<span class="ans">for** public transport.

---

<div class="pat">meet / satisfy / fulfil demand <b>for</b> + noun &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; respond <b>to</b> demand</div>

* **meet / satisfy demand for** &mdash; to provide enough of it.
* **fulfil demand** &mdash; British, slightly more formal.
* **respond to demand** &mdash; to react to it, not to satisfy it: "Suppliers responded to demand by increasing production".

*Tip: two prepositions to keep apart: <b>demand <b>for</b> X</b> and <b>respond <b>to</b> demand</b>.*
