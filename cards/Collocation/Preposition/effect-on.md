# Front

<div class="hint">PREPOSITION &middot; = consequence, often negative &middot; <b>on</b></div>

Type the preposition:

<span class="ex">"The new regulations had a negative effect ____________ small businesses."</span>

---

# Type

on, upon

---

# Back

The new regulations had a negative effect **<span class="ans">on** small businesses.

---

<div class="pat">have a positive / negative / adverse effect <b>on</b> + noun / -ing</div>

<span class="ex">Rising interest rates have had a detrimental effect on investment.</span>

*Tip: <b>effect on</b> vs <b>impact on**: the effect is the consequence (it may be small), the impact is the force of it. Both take <b>on</b>. A useful Task 2 frame: "This policy has a significant impact <b>on</b> …"*
