---

<div class="pat">reliance <b>on</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; dependence <b>on</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; a dependence <b>on</b> imports</div>

* **reliance on** &mdash; how much a country or person depends on something.
* **dependence on** &mdash; almost the same, slightly more formal: "the country's dependence on natural resources".

*Tip: <span class="no">reliance of fossil fuels</span> &middot; <span class="no">dependence of natural resources</span>. Task 2 frame: "…reduces a country's reliance on fossil fuels".*
