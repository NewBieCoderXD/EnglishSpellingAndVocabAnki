# Front

<div class="hint">PREPOSITION &middot; a heavy load, financial or practical</div>

Type the preposition:

<span class="ex">"Rising tuition fees place a heavy burden ____________ low-income families."</span>

---

# Type

on, upon

---

# Back

Rising tuition fees place a heavy burden **<span class="ans">on** low-income families.

---

<div class="pat">place / put a burden <b>on</b> + people / the budget / the environment</div>

<span class="ex">The policy places a considerable burden on public finances.</span>

*Tip: <b>burden on</b> is a pressure the subject inflicts; <b>burden of</b> is a burden defined by what causes it: "the burden of debt", "the burden of proof". Same for <b>pressure</b>: <i>pressure on</i> vs <i>pressure of demand</i>.*
