# Front

<div class="hint">PREPOSITION &middot; a change in something &middot; <span class="no">increase <b>of</b></span></div>

Type the preposition:

<span class="ex">"There was a significant increase ____________ demand for housing."</span>

---

# Type

in

---

# Back

There was a significant increase **<span class="ans">in</span>** demand for housing.

---

<div class="pat">an increase in / a rise in / a growth in / a decline in / a drop in / a reduction in + noun</div>

<span class="ex">There was a sharp rise in demand for energy.</span>

*Tip: the whole family takes **in**: <span class="no">an increase of the number of cars</span> is wrong. Use **of** only for a share of a whole: "70% of the total". This is the single most frequent preposition error in Task 1.*
