# Front

<div class="hint">PREPOSITION &middot; <span class="no">attribute X <b>with</b> Y</span></div>

Type the preposition:

<span class="ex">"The increase was largely attributed ____________ population growth."</span>

---

# Type

to

---

# Back

The increase was largely attributed **<span class="ans">to** population growth.

---

<div class="pat">attribute A <b>to</b> B &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; B is attributed <b>to</b> A</div>

<span class="ex">The decline was largely attributed to reduced consumer demand.</span>

*Tip: <span class="no">attributed with</span> &middot; <span class="no">attributed by</span> are both wrong in this frame. **To** marks the cause, in the active and the passive.*
