# Type

pressure, a strain, a burden, stress, a drain

# Back

Rapid urbanisation puts **<span class="ans">pressure</span>** on the country's natural resources.

---

<div class="pat">put <b>pressure / a strain / a burden</b> <b>on</b> + thing affected</div>

* **pressure on** &mdash; general strain: "put pressure on public services".
* **a strain on** &mdash; a burden that is hard to sustain: "put a strain on the health system".
* **a burden on** &mdash; a heavy, often financial load: "put a burden on taxpayers".
* **impact / effect on** &mdash; a consequence, not a strain: "have an impact on the environment".

*Tip: <span class="no">put pressure to</span> &middot; <span class="no">put a pressure on</span> &mdash; no article, and always **on**.*
