---

<div class="pat">alleviate / mitigate / ease <b>a problem / poverty / pressure</b></div>

* **alleviate** &mdash; to make a problem less severe: "alleviate poverty".
* **mitigate** &mdash; to reduce the harmful effect of something: "mitigate the effects of climate change".
* **ease** &mdash; to make something less painful: "ease the burden on hospitals".
* **reduce** &mdash; the neutral verb: "reduce costs".

*Tip: the natural pairing for a Task 2 body paragraph is <span class="ok">exacerbate</span> / <span class="ok">alleviate</span>.*
