# Front

<div class="hint">TASK2_PROBLEM &middot; past simple &middot; NOT with "the number"</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"The country ____________ a significant increase in population over the decade."</span>

---

# Type

experienced, saw, witnessed, recorded, underwent

---

# Back

The country **<span class="ans">experienced</span>** a significant increase in population over the decade.

---

<div class="pat">a country / sector / university <b>experienced</b> a rise / a decline / growth</div>

* **experienced** &mdash; formal and safe: "The sector experienced rapid growth."
* **saw / witnessed** &mdash; equally fine: "The country saw a sharp rise in demand."
* **recorded** &mdash; for figures: "The university recorded a fall in applications."

*Tip: <span class="no">The number experienced an increase.</span> A number cannot experience &mdash; it <b>increased</b>, or write <span class="ok">There was an increase in the number</span>. And do not force **experienced** into every sentence.*
