---

<div class="pat">an article / a report / a study <b>about</b> or <b>on</b> + topic</div>

* **about** &mdash; the everyday word: "a report about climate change".
* **on** &mdash; slightly more formal, and standard for published work: "a report on the economy".
* **regarding / concerning** &mdash; for *in relation to* inside a sentence, not as a label for a text.

*Tip: <b>about</b> is not "unacademic" &mdash; over-formalising is a bigger risk than sounding plain. Use **regarding** when you mean "in relation to", and **about** when you mean the subject of a piece of writing.*
