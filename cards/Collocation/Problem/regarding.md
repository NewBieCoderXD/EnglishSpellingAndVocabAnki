# Front

Type the missing word:

<span class="ex">"__________ the use of public transport, the figure increased steadily."</span>

# Type

Regarding, With regard to, With respect to, Concerning, In terms of, As for

---

# Back

**<span class="ans">Regarding</span>** the use of public transport, the figure increased steadily.

---

<div class="pat"><b>Regarding / With regard to</b> + noun, &hellip;</div>

* **Regarding / concerning / with regard to** &mdash; neutral, safe at the start of a sentence.
* **In terms of** &mdash; comparing aspects: "In terms of cost, public transport was cheaper."
* **As for / in the case of** &mdash; slightly informal.

*Tip: these are for **formal academic** writing. Ordinary English uses **about** freely: "an article about climate change". Do not replace every **about** with **regarding** &mdash; that is exactly the "academic for the sake of it" writing to avoid.*
