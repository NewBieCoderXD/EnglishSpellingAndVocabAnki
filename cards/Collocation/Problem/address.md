---

<div class="pat">address / tackle <b>an issue / a problem</b></div>

* **address** &mdash; to deal with a problem, formally: "The government must address the issue."
* **tackle** &mdash; to take action against a big problem: "Governments should tackle climate change."
* **resolve / solve** &mdash; to fix it completely.

*Tip: <span class="no">address with</span> is not a phrase. And do not confuse **address** with <b>account for</b> (70% of the total), which means "constitute" or "explain".*
