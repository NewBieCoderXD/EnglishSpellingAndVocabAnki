---

<div class="pat">something <b>exacerbates</b> a problem &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; a problem <b>worsens</b></div>

* **exacerbate** &mdash; the cause is the subject: "Plastic waste exacerbates environmental problems."
* **worsen** &mdash; the problem is the subject: "The problem has worsened."
* **aggravate / intensify** &mdash; formal synonyms of **exacerbate**.

*Tip: **exacerbate** does not mean "cause" on its own &mdash; something bad must already exist. Never "exacerbate" in the sense of <i>increase</i> (a number, an amount).*
