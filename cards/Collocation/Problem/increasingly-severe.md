# Back

Water scarcity has become increasingly **<span class="ans">severe</span>** in the region.

---

<div class="pat">become <b>increasingly</b> + adjective &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; a growing problem</div>

* increasingly severe / serious / acute &mdash; severity
* increasingly prevalent / common &mdash; how widespread it is
* increasingly difficult / challenging &mdash; difficulty

*Tip: **increasingly severe** for severity, **worsened** for a change over time, **exacerbated** for an action that makes it worse. They are not interchangeable, and they take different subjects.*
