# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_DATA &middot; "in general" &middot; <span class="no">Holistically, &hellip;</span></div>

Type the missing word:

<span class="ex">"__________, the number of users increased in every region."</span>

---

# Type

Overall, Altogether, On the whole, In general, Generally, Broadly

---

# Back

**<span class="ans">Overall</span>**, the number of users increased in every region.

---

<div class="pat"><b>Overall / On the whole / In general</b>, + main statement</div>

*Tip: sentence-initial summary words: **overall, in general, on the whole, broadly speaking, to sum up**. **Holistically** is not a substitute for <span class="ok">overall / on the whole / in general</span> &mdash; it describes treating something as a whole, not summarising.*
