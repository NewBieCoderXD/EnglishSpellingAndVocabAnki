---

<div class="pat">the problem <b>has worsened</b> / <b>has deteriorated</b></div>

* **worsen** &mdash; general: "The problem has worsened."
* **deteriorate** &mdash; formal: "Conditions deteriorated sharply."
* **intensify / escalate** &mdash; a conflict or pressure: "Tensions escalated."

*Tip: don't confuse the direction: the PROBLEM worsens; a POLICY exacerbates it. And never <span class="no">the problem has been worsen</span>.*
