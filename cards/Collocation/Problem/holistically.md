<div class="pat">consider X <b>holistically / as a whole</b></div>

**holistically** <span class="pos">adverb</span> &mdash; considering something as a whole, taking multiple aspects together

<span class="ex">Climate change should be addressed holistically, rather than sector by sector.</span>

*Tip: it does **NOT** mean "overall". <span class="no">Holistically, the number increased.</span> &mdash; for "in general" use <b>Overall</b> or <b>On the whole</b>. **Holistically** describes how you treat something, not the summary of a trend.*
