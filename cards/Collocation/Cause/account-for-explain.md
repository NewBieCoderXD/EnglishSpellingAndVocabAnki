# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_CAUSE &middot; <b>passive</b> &middot; the cause comes after it</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"The gap between the two figures can be ____________ higher demand for housing."</span>

---

# Type

accounted for, attributed to, explained by, explained, due to, put down to

---

# Back

The gap between the two figures can be **<span class="ans">accounted for</span>** higher demand for housing.

---

<div class="pat">difference <b>can be accounted for</b> + cause</div>

**account for** <span class="pos">verb</span> &mdash; <b>meaning 2: explain</b> (passive only)

<span class="ex">The discrepancy can be accounted for by a change in methodology.</span>

*Tip: the two meanings of **account for** are unrelated &mdash; <span class="no">constitute</span> 70% of something vs <span class="no">explain</span> something. In the passive it can only mean "explain", and the cause always follows. Add <span class="no">largely / mainly / partly</span> to soften it.*
