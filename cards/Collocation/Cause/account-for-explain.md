# Type

# Back

The gap between the two figures can be **<span class="ans">accounted for by</span>** higher demand for housing.

---

<div class="pat">difference <b>can be accounted for by</b> + cause</div>

**account for** <span class="pos">verb</span> &mdash; <b>meaning 2: explain</b> (passive only)

<span class="ex">The discrepancy can be accounted for by a change in methodology.</span>

*Tip: the two meanings of **account for** are unrelated &mdash; <span class="no">constitute</span> 70% of something vs <span class="no">explain</span> something. In the passive it can only mean "explain", and the cause comes after <b>by</b>: <span class="ok">can be accounted for by a change in methodology</span> &mdash; <span class="no">can be accounted for a change in methodology</span> and <span class="no">can be explained a change in methodology</span> are not English. <b>Attribute</b> takes <b>to</b> instead, <b>blame</b> takes <b>on</b>. Add <span class="no">largely / mainly / partly</span> to soften it.*
