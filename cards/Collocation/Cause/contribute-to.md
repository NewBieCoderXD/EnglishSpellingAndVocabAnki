# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_CAUSE &middot; verb + <b>to</b> &middot; one cause among several</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"Rising house prices ____________ the widening gap between rich and poor."</span>

---

# Type

contributed to, played a role in, was a factor in, added to, resulted in, led to, was partly responsible for

---

# Back

Rising house prices **<span class="ans">contributed to</span>** the widening gap between rich and poor.

---

<div class="pat">X <b>contributes to</b> + noun / -ing</div>

**contribute to** <span class="pos">verb</span> &mdash; to be one of several causes

<span class="ex">Several factors contributed to the decline in literacy rates.</span>

*Tip: always <b>to</b> &mdash; <span class="no">contributed for</span> is wrong. After <b>to</b> use a noun or an <b>-ing</b> form: "contributed to <i>the rise</i>" / "contributed to <i>reducing</i> poverty".*
