Two directions, one fact. Which verbs go with which?

---

# Back

<span class="ex">"Higher demand resulted in an increase in prices."</span>
&harr;
<span class="ex">"The increase in prices resulted from higher demand."</span>

---

<div class="pat">CAUSE &rarr; EFFECT<br>X <b>leads to</b> Y<br>X <b>results in</b> Y<br>X <b>contributes to</b> Y<br>X <b>causes</b> Y<br>X <b>gives rise to</b> Y</div>

<div class="pat">EFFECT &rarr; CAUSE<br>Y <b>results from</b> X<br>Y <b>is attributed to</b> X<br>Y <b>was caused by</b> X<br>Y <b>stems from</b> X<br>Y <b>is due to</b> X</div>

*Tip: subject-first = the cause; subject-last = the effect. When you are unsure, state the fact the other way round.*
