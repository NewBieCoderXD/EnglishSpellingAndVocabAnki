# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_CAUSE &middot; the <b>effect</b> is the subject &middot; the cause follows</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"The increase in housing prices ____________ population growth."</span>

---

# Type

resulted from, stemmed from, arose from, was due to, resulted directly from, was driven by

---

# Back

The increase in housing prices **<span class="ans">resulted from</span>** population growth.

---

<div class="pat">Y <b>results from</b> X &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; the effect comes first</div>

**result from** <span class="pos">verb</span> &mdash; to happen because of X

<span class="ex">The increase resulted from higher consumer demand.</span>

*Tip: turn the sentence round and the verb changes &mdash; see the next card. If in doubt about the direction, rewrite the sentence with the cause first and use <b>led to</b>.*
