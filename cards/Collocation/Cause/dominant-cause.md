# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_CAUSE &middot; one word &middot; the <b>strongest</b> cause</div>

Type the missing word:

<span class="ex">"Commercial farming was the ____________ cause of deforestation."</span>

---

# Type

dominant, main, leading, principal, primary, most significant

---

# Back

Commercial farming was the **<span class="ans">dominant</span>** cause of deforestation.

---

<div class="pat">the dominant / main / leading cause <b>of</b> + problem</div>

<span class="ex">The leading cause of the decline was reduced consumer demand.</span>

*Tip: don't stack intensifiers &mdash; <span class="no">the most dominant reason</span>. **Dominant** already means "the strongest". For the opposite, say <span class="ok">the least significant cause</span> or <span class="ok">the smallest proportion</span>.*
