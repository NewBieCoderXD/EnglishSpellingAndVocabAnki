# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_CAUSE &middot; the <b>cause</b> is the subject &middot; the effect follows</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"Population growth ____________ a sharp rise in housing prices."</span>

---

# Type

resulted in, led to, gave rise to, brought about, caused, triggered, spurred

---

# Back

Population growth **<span class="ans">resulted in</span>** a sharp rise in housing prices.

---

<div class="pat">X <b>results in / leads to</b> Y &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; the cause comes first</div>

**result in / lead to** <span class="pos">verb</span> &mdash; to make Y happen

<span class="ex">Higher consumer demand resulted in an increase in prices.</span>

*Tip: <b>result from</b> &harr; <b>result in</b> is the same fact with the words reversed: "A resulted in B" = "B resulted from A". <b>caused by</b> &harr; <b>caused</b>.*
