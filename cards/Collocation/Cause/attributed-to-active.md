# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_CAUSE &middot; active &middot; <b>to</b> + cause</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"The company ____________ the failure of the project to poor communication."</span>

---

# Type

attributed, put down, blamed, traced, ascribed

---

# Back

The company **<span class="ans">attributed</span>** the failure of the project to poor communication.

---

<div class="pat">attribute A <b>to</b> B &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; A = the effect, B = the cause</div>

**attribute** <span class="pos">verb</span> &mdash; to say that A was caused by B

<span class="ex">Analysts attributed the decline in sales to reduced consumer confidence.</span>

*Tip: <span class="no">attribute A with B</span> is never right. Passive flips the order: B is the subject, so you need <b>attributed to</b> + B.*
