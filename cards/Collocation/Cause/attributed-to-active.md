# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_CAUSE &middot; active &middot; <b>to</b> + cause &middot; type the verb <i>and</i> the preposition</div>

Type the missing verb + preposition:

<span class="ex">"The company ____________ the failure of the project <b>to</b> poor communication."</span>

---

# Type

attributed to, put down to, ascribed to, traced to

---

# Back

The company **<span class="ans">attributed</span>** the failure of the project **<span class="ans">to</span>** poor communication.

---

<div class="pat">attribute A <b>to</b> B &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; A = the effect, B = the cause</div>

**attribute** <span class="pos">verb</span> &mdash; to say that A was caused by B

<span class="ex">Analysts attributed the decline in sales to reduced consumer confidence.</span>

*Tip: <span class="no">attribute A with B</span> is never right, and <b>blame</b> does not belong in this frame either: <span class="no">blamed the failure to poor communication</span> &mdash; <b>blame</b> takes <b>on</b> or <b>for</b>, as in <span class="ok">blamed the failure on poor communication</span> / <span class="ok">blamed poor communication for the failure</span>. The preposition belongs to the verb and comes after the object, so type <b>attributed to</b> even though the sentence shows the <b>to</b>. Passive flips the order: B is the subject, so you need <b>attributed to</b> + B.*
