# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_CAUSE &middot; passive &middot; the <b>effect</b> is the subject</div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"The increase in the number of vehicles can be ____________ population growth."</span>

---

# Type

attributed to, ascribed to, explained by, put down to, due to, accounted for

---

# Back

The increase in the number of vehicles can be **<span class="ans">attributed to</span>** population growth.

---

<div class="pat">effect <b>can be attributed to</b> + cause</div>

**attribute A to B** <span class="pos">verb</span> &mdash; to say that A resulted from B

<span class="ex">The increase was largely attributed to higher consumer demand.</span>

*Tip: "largely / mainly / partly attributed to" is the natural way to hedge, and it is Task 1 gold. The cause is what follows <b>to</b>.*
