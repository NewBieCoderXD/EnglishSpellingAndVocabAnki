# Front

<div class="hint">TASK1_CAUSE &middot; verb + <b>a role</b> + <b>in</b></div>

Type the missing verb:

<span class="ex">"Improved public transport ____________ a significant role in reducing congestion."</span>

---

# Type

played, had

---

# Back

Improved public transport **<span class="ans">played</span>** a significant role in reducing congestion.

---

<div class="pat">play / have <b>a role / a part in</b> + noun / -ing</div>

**play a role in** <span class="pos">verb phrase</span> &mdash; to be one contributing factor

<span class="ex">Population growth played a significant role in the increase in demand.</span>

*Tip: <b>in</b>, not <span class="no">of</span>. Adjectives that fit: significant, important, key, crucial, major.*
