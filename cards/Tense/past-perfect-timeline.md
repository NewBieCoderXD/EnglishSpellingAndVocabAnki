# Front

<div class="hint">Past perfect &middot; two past events, the earlier one first</div>

Type the verb form:

<span class="ex">"By the time I arrived, the train __________."</span>

---

# Type

had left, had already left

# Back

By the time I arrived, the train **<span class="ans">had left</span>**.

Use the past perfect (<b>had + past participle</b>) when you need to show that <b>one past action happened before another past action or past time marker</b>.

The train left first; arriving came second. The past perfect sets the earlier event behind the later one.

<div class="pat">earlier past event &rarr; <b>had + past participle</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; later past event &rarr; simple past</div>

With only one past event the simple past is enough: <span class="ex">"The train left at 6 a.m."</span>
