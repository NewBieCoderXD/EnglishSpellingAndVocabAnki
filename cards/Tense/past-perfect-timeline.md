# Front

<div class="hint">Past perfect &middot; timeline</div>

When should you use the past perfect (<b>had + past participle</b>) instead of the simple past?

---

# Back

When you need to show that <b>one past action happened before another past action or past time marker</b>.

<span class="ex">"By the time I arrived, the train had left."</span>

The train left first; arriving came second. The past perfect sets the earlier event behind the later one.

<div class="pat">earlier past event &rarr; <b>had + past participle</b> &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; later past event &rarr; simple past</div>
