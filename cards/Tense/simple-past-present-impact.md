# Front

<div class="hint">Simple past &middot; present impact, but a finished time marker</div>

Type the verb form:

<span class="ex">"I __________ my leg yesterday, so I still cannot walk today."</span>

---

# Type

broke

---

# Back

I **<span class="ans">broke</span>** my leg yesterday, so I still cannot walk today.

<span class="ok">The sentence is correct with the simple past.</span> Even though there is a present impact (<b>I still cannot walk</b>), it also gives an explicit <b>finished time marker</b> — <b>yesterday</b>. A dated, finished event anchors the verb firmly in the simple past, and the present result is stated separately ("I still cannot walk today").

<div class="pat">finished time marker (<b>yesterday</b>, <b>in 2020</b>, <b>last year</b>) &rarr; <b>simple past</b></div>

<span class="no">have broken</span> is wrong next to <b>yesterday</b>. Drop the time marker too and <span class="no">I have broken my leg, so I still cannot walk</span> becomes correct.
