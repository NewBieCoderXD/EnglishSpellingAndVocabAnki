# Back

<b><span class="ok">It is correct as written.</span></b>

Even though there is a present impact (<b>I still cannot walk</b>), the sentence also gives an explicit <b>finished time marker</b> — <b>yesterday</b>. A dated, finished event anchors the verb firmly in the simple past, and the present result is stated separately ("I still cannot walk today").

<div class="pat">finished time marker (<b>yesterday</b>, <b>in 2020</b>, <b>last year</b>) &rarr; <b>simple past</b></div>
