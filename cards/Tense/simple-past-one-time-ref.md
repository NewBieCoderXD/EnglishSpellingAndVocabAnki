# Front

<div class="hint">Simple past &middot; one time reference</div>

Correct or incorrect?

<span class="ex">"In 2020, the company launched the product."</span>

Only one time reference is given (<b>In 2020</b>). Is a perfect tense required?

---

# Back

<b><span class="ok">Correct</span> — the simple past is fine.</b>

Even with a single time reference, the simple past (<b>launched</b>) is used because it is a <b>finished action at a specific past time</b> — it does not lead up to another point in the past.

<div class="pat">finished action + specific past time &rarr; <b>simple past</b></div>

No perfect tense is needed: nothing connects the event to a later past moment or to the present.
