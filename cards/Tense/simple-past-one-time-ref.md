# Front

<div class="hint">Spot the error &middot; a finished time marker (<b>in 2020</b>)</div>

This sentence uses the wrong tense. Type the correct verb form:

<span class="ex">"In 2020, the company <span class="no"><u>has launched</u></span> the product."</span>

---

# Type

launched

---

# Back

In 2020, the company **<span class="ans">launched</span>** the product.

<span class="no">has launched</span> is wrong here: <b>In 2020</b> is a finished time marker, so the event is closed and the simple past is required.

Even with a single time reference the simple past (<b>launched</b>) works, because it is a <b>finished action at a specific past time</b> — it does not lead up to another point in the past.

<div class="pat">finished action + specific past time &rarr; <b>simple past</b></div>

No perfect tense is needed: nothing connects the event to a later past moment or to the present. Keep the present perfect for an open period instead — "The company <b>has launched</b> three products this year."
