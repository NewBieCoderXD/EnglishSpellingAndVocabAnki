# Front

<div class="hint">Present perfect vs. simple past &middot; life experience</div>

Why use the present perfect in <span class="ex">"I have visited Japan three times"</span> instead of the simple past?

---

# Back

Because it treats your trips as part of your <b>life experience up to now</b> — they connect your past to the person you are today. The present perfect does not say <i>when</i>; it says "as of now, this is true of me."

With the simple past, <span class="ex">"I visited Japan three times"</span> sounds like a <b>closed chapter</b>, usually tied to a specific period that is over.

<div class="pat">experience up to now &rarr; present perfect &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; specific finished trip &rarr; simple past</div>
