<div class="w">put</div>
<div class="phon">/pʊt/</div>
<div class="pos">full vowel → /ʊ/ "short oo"</div>

<p>A lone <b>u</b> = /ʊ/ in a handful of words: put, pull, full, push, bush, cushion.</p>

<span class="ex">Almost every other stressed <b>u</b> is /ʌ/ (cup) or /uː/ (flu) — these few are /ʊ/.</span>
