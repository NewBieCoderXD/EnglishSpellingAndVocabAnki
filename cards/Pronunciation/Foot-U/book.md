<div class="pos">full vowel → /ʊ/ "short oo"</div>

<p><b>oo</b> = /ʊ/ here, the "short oo" in <b>book</b>, <b>good</b>, <b>foot</b> — not /uː/.</p>

<span class="ex">Distinguish: <b>book</b> /bʊk/ vs <b>hoot</b> /huːt/. /ʊ/ is relaxed, /uː/ is long and tense.</span>
