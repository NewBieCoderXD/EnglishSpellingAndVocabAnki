<div class="w">pull vs pool</div>

<p>1. <b>pull</b> /pʊl/ — /ʊ/ "short oo" · 2. <b>pool</b> /puːl/ — /uː/ "long oo".</p>

<p>The opening consonant and ending are identical: /p_l/ — the vowel length is the only difference.</p>

<span class="ex">Pull /pʊl/ the door shut; dive into the pool /puːl/.</span>
