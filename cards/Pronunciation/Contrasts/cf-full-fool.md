# Back

<div class="w">full vs fool</div>

<p>1. <b>full</b> /fʊl/ — /ʊ/ "short oo" · 2. <b>fool</b> /fuːl/ — /uː/ "long oo".</p>

<p>Same <b>ul</b>, different vowel length. /ʊ/ is short and near, /uː/ is long and back.</p>

<span class="ex">The glass is <b>full</b> /fʊl/; only a <b>fool</b> /fuːl/ would spill it.</span>
