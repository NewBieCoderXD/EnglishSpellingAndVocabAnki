# Back

<div class="w">cup vs coop</div>

<p>1. <b>cup</b> /kʌp/ — /ʌ/ "uh" · 2. <b>coop</b> /kuːp/ — /uː/ "long oo".</p>

<p>The length and lip-rounding separate them: /uː/ is held longer and the lips are rounded throughout.</p>

<span class="ex">Say "coop" and the /uː/ hangs on; in "cup" the /ʌ/ snaps off immediately.</span>
