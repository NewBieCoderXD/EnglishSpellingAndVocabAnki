# Back

<div class="w">cud vs could</div>

<p>1. <b>cud</b> /kʌd/ — /ʌ/ "uh" · 2. <b>could</b> /kʊd/ — /ʊ/ "short oo" with a silent <b>l</b>.</p>

<p>A near-minimal pair: the spelled <b>ou</b> in "could" sounds like the <b>u</b> in "cud" — but shorter.</p>

<span class="ex">cud rhymes with mud /mʌd/ and bud /bʌd/; could rhymes with wood /wʊd/.</span>
