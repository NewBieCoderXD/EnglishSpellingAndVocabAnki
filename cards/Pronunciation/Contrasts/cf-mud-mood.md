<div class="w">mud vs mood</div>

<p>1. <b>mud</b> /mʌd/ — /ʌ/ "uh" · 2. <b>mood</b> /muːd/ — /uː/ "long oo".</p>

<p>A true minimal pair — only the vowel differs. Four spellings, four sounds, same "look": m_u_d vs m_oo_d.</p>

<span class="ex">Mood swings: <b>mood</b> /muːd/ is long; stepping in <b>mud</b> /mʌd/ is short and glum.</span>
