# Back

<div class="w">supply vs sun</div>

<p>1. <b>supply</b> /səˈplaɪ/ — first syllable is a lazy schwa /ə/, not /ʌ/ · 2. <b>sun</b> /sʌn/ — a full /ʌ/.</p>

<p>Learners often "fill in" the first syllable of supply with /ʌ/ — but schwa is exactly the <i>absence</i> of a full vowel.</p>

<span class="ex">Think "sə-PLY" — the tiny first syllable is the schwa; "sun" is a loud, full /sʌn/.</span>
