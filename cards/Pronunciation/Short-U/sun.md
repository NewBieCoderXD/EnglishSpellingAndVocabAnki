<div class="w">sun</div>
<div class="phon">/sʌn/</div>
<div class="pos">full vowel → /ʌ/ "uh"</div>

<p>Stressed <b>u</b> = /ʌ/, the "uh" as in <b>cup</b>. It's a full, tensed sound — not a lazy schwa.</p>

<span class="ex">Contrast: <b>supply</b> /səˈplaɪ/ — the stressed "ply" is fine, but its <b>u-</b> prefix is schwa.</span>
