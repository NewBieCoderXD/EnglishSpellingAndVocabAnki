<div class="w">but</div>
<div class="phon">/bʌt/</div>
<div class="pos">full vowel → /ʌ/ "uh"</div>

<p>In isolation it's a full /ʌ/. But in fast sentences the word <b>but</b> is usually unstressed and reduces to /bət/ — a schwa.</p>

<span class="ex">The very word <b>but</b> demonstrates the schwa-vs-/ʌ/ contrast: stressed /bʌt/, unstressed /bət/.</span>
