<div class="phon">/əˈləʊn/</div>
<div class="pos">first syllable → schwa</div>

<p>The letter <b>a</b> at the start of a word is often unstressed /ə/: alone, about, afford, across.</p>

<span class="ex">The word <b>one</b> /wʌn/ is different — a stressed single syllable with a full <b>/ʌ/</b>.</span>
