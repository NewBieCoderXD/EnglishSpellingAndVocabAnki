<div class="phon">/səˈplaɪ/</div>
<div class="pos">first syllable → schwa</div>

<p>The first letter <b>u</b> sounds nothing like "u" — unstressed <b>u</b> is just /ə/.</p>

<span class="ex">The word <b>sun</b> /sʌn/ has a stressed <b>u</b> = /ʌ/ — a full sound, not a schwa.</span>
