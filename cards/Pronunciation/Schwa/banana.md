<div class="w">banana</div>
<div class="phon">/bəˈnɑːnə/</div>
<div class="pos">syllables 1 and 3 → schwa</div>

<p>Only the stressed "na" keeps its sound; the two <b>a</b>s around it are both schwa: bə-NA-nə.</p>

<span class="ex">Schwa can appear in the middle <i>and</i> at the end — the stressed syllable is the only "real" vowel.</span>
